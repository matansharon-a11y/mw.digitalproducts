FAQs

Plugin or presets: what's the difference?

The Lightroom Classic plugin sorts, keywords, edits and exports a whole shoot inside Lightroom Classic 13 or newer, and MWP Presets are included with it. MWP Presets alone are 50 presets and 33 LUTs for Lightroom Classic, Lightroom, Lightroom Mobile and Camera Raw. Buy the plugin and you get both; buy the presets and you get the presets.

Which programs do they work in?

Lightroom Classic, Lightroom (desktop), Lightroom Mobile and Photoshop Camera Raw. Lightroom on its own is enough. The LUTs work in Photoshop, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and Final Cut Pro. The 12 adaptive presets need Lightroom Classic 11+, Lightroom 5+ or Camera Raw 14+.

Which Lightroom does the plugin work in?

Lightroom Classic 13 or newer, on Mac or Windows. It doesn't work in Lightroom (the cloud app), Lightroom Mobile or Photoshop. For Photoshop there's the MWP Photoshop Plugin Panel.

What's in the download?

MWP Presets: 50 desktop presets (.xmp) for Lightroom Classic, Lightroom and Camera Raw, 50 mobile files (.dng) for Lightroom Mobile, 33 LUTs (.cube), video tutorials and an install guide. The Lightroom Classic plugin: the plugin for Lightroom Classic 13 or newer, with built-in help and a video tutorial.

Will they work on my photos, not only on yours?

The 12 adaptive presets use Lightroom's AI masks to find the subject, the sky and the background in each photo, so they adjust to your frame. Every photo is different: expect to set white balance and exposure after the preset, as in the examples on this page.

Can I use them on my phone only?

Yes. The pack includes 50 DNG files for Lightroom Mobile on iPhone, iPad and Android. If you also use Lightroom on desktop, install there and they sync to your phone.

Can I use the LUTs on video?

Yes. They carry the colour and tone of presets 01 to 33 and are built for standard Rec.709 footage. With LOG footage, convert to Rec.709 first, then apply the LUT.

How do I get and install the files?

Right after payment they're waiting in My downloads, in your MWP account, with a video tutorial. The pack includes an install guide with a page for each program. Most installs take a couple of minutes. The plugin is added once in Lightroom Classic: File › Plug-in Manager › Add.

What can I do with the files?

Use them on all your own photos and videos, including paid client work, on every device you use. The files are for you alone: please don't share, resell or repackage them.

Can I get a refund?

If the presets or the plugin don't work for you, write to support within 14 days of purchase with your order number. We'll help you get them working, and if we can't, we'll refund you. The full terms are on our Terms page.
