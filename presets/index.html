FAQs

Will they work on my photos, not only on yours?

The 12 adaptive presets use Lightroom's AI masks to find the subject, the sky and the background in each photo, so they adjust to your frame. Every photo is different: expect to set white balance and exposure after the preset, as in the examples on this page.

Which programs do they work in?

Lightroom Classic, Lightroom (desktop), Lightroom Mobile and Photoshop Camera Raw. Lightroom on its own is enough. The LUTs work in Photoshop, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and Final Cut Pro.

What is the Lightroom Classic plugin?

A plugin that comes with MWP Presets and works inside Lightroom Classic 13 or newer, on Mac or Windows. It follows a whole shoot: cull bursts, organise trips and species, edit the animal, the sky or the background, and export. It's in early access: it works today, and new features arrive as free updates in My downloads. Everything it does is ordinary Lightroom settings, masks and collections, so nothing is locked in. Once installed, its window in Lightroom is titled MWP Panel.

Presets or Panel: which do I need?

If you edit in Lightroom, MWP Presets: 50 presets, plus a Lightroom Classic plugin. If you open your photos in Photoshop, MWP Panel. If you use both, the Presets + Panel bundle gives you the same look in each and costs less than buying them separately.

Can I use them on my phone only?

Yes. The pack includes 50 DNG files for Lightroom Mobile on iPhone, iPad and Android. If you also use Lightroom on desktop, install there and they sync to your phone.

Do they work on JPEG?

Yes. They shine on RAW files, which give them the most room to work, and on JPEGs they give a gentler version of the look.

Can I use the LUTs on video?

Yes. They carry the colour and tone of presets 01 to 33 and are built for standard Rec.709 footage. With LOG footage, convert to Rec.709 first, then apply the LUT.

How do I get and install the files?

Right after payment they're waiting in My downloads, in your MWP account, with a video tutorial. The pack includes an install guide with a page for each program. Most installs take a couple of minutes.

What can I do with the files?

Use them on all your own photos and videos, including paid client work, on every device you use. The files are for you alone: please don’t share, resell or repackage them.

Can I get a refund?

If the presets don’t work for you, write to support within 14 days of purchase with your order number. We’ll help you get them working, and if we can’t, we’ll refund you. The full terms are on our Terms page.
