FAQs

Which programs do they work in?

Lightroom Classic, Lightroom (desktop), Lightroom Mobile and Photoshop Camera Raw. Lightroom on its own is enough. The LUTs work in Photoshop, Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and Final Cut Pro.

Can I use them on my phone only?

Yes. The pack includes 50 DNG files for Lightroom Mobile on iPhone, iPad and Android. If you also use Lightroom on desktop, install there and they sync to your phone.

Do they work on JPEG?

Yes. They shine on RAW files, which give them the most room to work, and on JPEGs they give a gentler version of the look.

Can I use the LUTs on video?

Yes. They carry the colour and tone of presets 01 to 33 and are built for standard Rec.709 footage. With LOG footage, convert to Rec.709 first, then apply the LUT.

How do I get and install the files?

Right after payment they're waiting in My downloads, in your MWP account. The pack includes an install guide with a page for each program. Most installs take a couple of minutes.

What can I do with the files?

Use them on all your own photos and videos, including paid client work, on every device you use. The files are for you alone: please don’t share, resell or repackage them.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days if you haven’t downloaded the files yet, or if a technical problem can’t be solved together. Write to contact@matanwildphotography.com with your order number.
