SUBJECT BACKGROUND
12 adaptive presets

Lightroom finds the animal. The preset lights it.

The adaptive presets use Lightroom's AI masks to find the subject, the sky or the background in each photo and treat each one on its own.

  • Subject: shadows and whites lifted on the animal
  • Sky: highlights held, deeper and calmer
  • Background: gently darker, so the animal stands out
