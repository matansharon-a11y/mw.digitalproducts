FAQs

Do I need to know layers and masks?

No. You choose what to edit and an effect, and the panel builds the layers and masks for you. A 21-page manual and a guide inside the panel explain every button from the start.

Presets or Panel: which do I need?

If you edit in Lightroom, MWP Presets. If you open your photos in Photoshop, MWP Panel. If you use both, the Presets + Panel bundle gives you the same look in each and costs less than buying them separately.

Which Photoshop do I need?

Photoshop 2023 (version 24.2) or newer, on Mac or Windows, from a Creative Cloud subscription. It does not run in Lightroom, on iPad or on phones.

Can it ruin my photo?

No. Every button adds its own layer and your original layer is locked. One Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on Mac) undoes a whole button, Reset MWP removes everything the panel added, and exports are always saved as new files.

Can I edit a whole series the same way?

Yes. Open the other photos, return to the edited one and click Apply under More photos. Subject, sky and background are found again on each. You can also save your edit as a look under Save my look.

Are updates included?

Yes. Updates to MWP Panel are free: new versions appear in My downloads.

License, computers and refunds?

A personal licence for your own work and client work. MWP Panel works on up to 3 computers at a time, signed in with your email; changed computer? Free one up in My downloads. If it doesn't work for you, write to support within 14 days of purchase: we'll help, and if we can't, we'll refund you.

What if I get stuck?

The Installation Guide covers common problems and their fixes, and every button in the panel has a tip and a help entry. Still stuck? Write to support and a person replies within one business day.
