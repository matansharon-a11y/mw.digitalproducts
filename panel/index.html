06Where it comes from

The questions from our evenings in camp.

On our expeditions the same editing questions come up at the end of the day: how to lift the animal, calm the sky, keep the colors true. MWP Panel puts our answers into Photoshop, simple enough for a first edit.

One slider. Most edits look best between 30 and 70 percent.
