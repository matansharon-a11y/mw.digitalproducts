FAQs

Do I need to know layers and masks?

No. You choose what to edit and an effect, and the plugin builds the layers and masks for you. A 21-page manual and a guide inside the plugin explain every button from the start.

MWP Presets or the Photoshop plugin: which do I need?

If you edit in Lightroom Classic, the Lightroom Classic plugin, with MWP Presets included; for the presets alone, MWP Presets. If you finish your photos in Photoshop, MWP Photoshop Plugin Panel. If you use both, the Lightroom Plugin + Photoshop Plugin Panel bundle gives you all of it for less, and the full set adds the Field Guides.

Which Photoshop do I need?

Photoshop 2023 (version 24.2) or newer, on Mac or Windows, from a Creative Cloud subscription. It does not run in Lightroom, on iPad or on phones. Working in Lightroom Classic? There's the Lightroom Classic plugin, with MWP Presets included.

Can it ruin my photo?

No. Every button adds its own layer and your original layer is locked. One Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on Mac) undoes a whole step, Reset MWP removes everything the plugin added, and exports are always saved as new files.

Can I edit a whole series the same way?

Yes. Open the other photos, return to the edited one and click Apply under More photos. Subject, sky and background are found again on each. You can also save your edit as a look under Save my look.

Are updates included?

Yes. Updates to MWP Photoshop Plugin Panel are free: new versions appear in My downloads.

Are there video tutorials?

Yes. Every purchase includes video tutorials for the plugin. You find them in My downloads, next to your files.

Licence, computers and refunds?

A personal licence for your own work and client work. MWP Photoshop Plugin Panel works on up to 3 computers at a time, signed in with your email; changed computer? Free one up in My downloads. If it doesn't work for you, write to support within 14 days of purchase: we'll help, and if we can't, we'll refund you.

What if I need help?

Start with the video tutorials in My downloads. The Installation Guide covers common problems and their fixes, and every button in the plugin has a tip and a help entry. Still need help? Write to support and a person replies within one business day.
