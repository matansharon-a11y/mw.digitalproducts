02What we invite you to do

We want you to use the tools freely and show what they can do. You're welcome to:

  • Edit and publish your photos made with the panel and presets
  • Share before/after comparisons and editing walkthroughs
  • Post feed posts, reels, stories and carousels about your workflow
  • Create Instagram collaborative posts with mw_phototours
  • Write about your honest experience: reviews, blog posts, newsletters, YouTube

What we ask in return: during your first 3 months, at least 3 posts and 1 optional reel per month, plus at least 1 story per week, all tagging MWP as described in section 3. After that, for the rest of the year, simply tag and mention us whenever you use the tools and are happy with the results. These are minimums: if you love the results, we'd love to see you share more of everything. Your opinions are your own. We never ask for a positive review, only an honest one.

03Tagging and disclosure

When you publish work made with the panel or presets:

Because you received the tools for free, your audience must know that. Feedback is a core part of the program. Ambassadors help us find issues, fix them and shape what comes next. As an ambassador, you commit to:

  • Share honest feedback on the panel and presets: what works, what's missing, what you'd change
  • Report bugs and glitches to our team as soon as you hit them, with a screenshot and your version and setup, so we can run fixes quickly
