FAQs

What exactly do I get?

All four guides, in English or Hebrew: you choose one language. Each guide has one-page lessons with a photo, a drawing and a short explanation, plus tools and diagrams, a cheat sheet, drills and a checklist. They open on any computer, tablet or phone.

Who are the guides for?

Everyone, from beginners to experienced photographers. The lessons start from the basics, and every lesson ends with "Break the rule" for those who want to go further. Start with any guide and any page.

Do I need a particular camera or gear?

No. The guides teach you to see and plan: composition, colour, light and planning. They don't depend on any camera or lens.

Do the guides cover editing?

No. They're about what happens before the click: composition, colour, light and planning. For the edit, the Lightroom Classic plugin, MWP Presets and MWP Photoshop Plugin Panel carry the same approach into Lightroom and Photoshop, and all of them come in bundles with the guides.

Can I buy just one guide?

Not at the moment. The four guides come as one set, in the language you choose, and together they cost less than four separate guides would.

How do I get the files?

Your purchased files will be available in the My downloads section.

What is the refund policy, and how can I use the files?

If the guides don't work for you, write to support within 14 days of purchase. We'll help, and if we can't, we'll refund you. The files are for your own use: please don't share, resell or give them away. The full terms are on our Terms page.
