Morning invoices

Every paid sale gets two documents in Morning: a tax invoice-receipt and a work order that names it. Test orders never do. Each order's documents are also under Sales → Orders.

For new sales

How the documents are filled in
  • Language: Hebrew for customers who use the shop in Hebrew, otherwise English. The amount and currency the customer paid (US dollars); Morning converts to shekels itself.
  • One line: the product's name (digital download). Buyers in Israel: VAT included in the price. Buyers abroad: VAT exempt (0%).
  • Payment: credit card, dated the day of the order. Remarks: the Lemon Squeezy order number.
  • The customer's name and country; their email only when the box above is ticked. They aren't added to Morning's client list.
  • A refunded order isn't cancelled automatically: cancel its invoice-receipt in Morning with a credit note.

These choices (VAT, payment type) are to be confirmed by Matan with the accountant before "Issue automatically" is switched on.
